Background

Lion Energy develops sustainable energy storage solutions.

Lion Energy was running short on time. Without an effective solution to empower installers, partnerships would continue falling apart.

Lion Energy empowers third party partners to hire installers that can bring energy solutions into established and new residential homes each year.


Technicians were struggling with the complex process of programming and commissioning home battery units on-site. This was a pressing issue that needed addressing in order to expand Lion Energy's reach and increase revenue.

What should have been a 10-minute setup often took hours, resulting in repeat visits, broken product, and frustrated partners.

Problem

Lion Energy was losing both existing and potential partnerships at a rapid rate.

The original commissioning experience existed only as a web-based application designed for use on laptops. However, in the field, most technicians worked exclusively on mobile devices. Expecting partnerships to provide and maintain laptops added friction, costs, and ultimately caused lost deals.


To further business implications, incorrect system setup was unacceptably common. This resulted in multiple field visits to the same home - to fix configuration errors. Each error meant wasted time, additional labor costs, and a poor experience for both the technician and homeowner.

Through this I came to fully understand the environment, the challenges, and the situations technicians experience during inverter and battery installation.

Testing

As an advocate for research, I pushed for testing to be conducted with field technicians. Unfortunately, this was denied multiple times due to budget constraints. I conducted internal usability walkthroughs with in-house engineers, product managers, and Spanish-speaking support staff who closely understood field conditions.

Even within the limits, we were able to identify usability gaps early in the process and refine accordingly.

We tested both low-fidelity clickthroughs and interactive high-fidelity prototypes, focusing on error prevention, comprehension, and flow speed.

Iteration

After launch, I have continued to refine the product based on post-release feedback from internal experts and partner installers. Small UI tweaks, clearer diagram labeling, and improved translation consistency were released in follow-up updates.

Solution

The solution centered around on three strategic changes

I designed a mobile-first application that transformed the complex commissioning process into a simple, guided, step-by-step experience comprehensive for electricians and homeowners alike. The app walks technicians through the entire commissioning flow, from initial setup to final verification. Each step includes expected device states, troubleshooting guidance, and validation checks to prevent errors before they occur.

Mobile Design

Mobile application optimized tools for field use, eliminating the need for laptops and the extra expenses that came with.

Visual Guidance

Specific visual diagrams offered technicians support through the 16 potential set-up scenarios.

Bilingual Support

Full Spanish language support enabled Lion Energy to reach a wider network of field technicians.

Looking back on the project, there are several lessons I learned to carry forward into future work.

What Went Well

  • Clear visual language and bilingual translation system
  • Decreased installation time through instructions
  • Customizing instructions to match various set-up scenarios

What Could Have Been Better

  • Memory storage within the Figma file kept filling up
  • Earlier involvement with engineering to streamline handoff process
  • Budget constraints prevented testing on real field technicians

Key Takeaways

  • Good design directly impacts business outcomes
  • Constraints are opportunities for creative problem solving
  • Good documentation promotes efficiency
